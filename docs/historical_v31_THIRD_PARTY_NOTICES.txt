This local preparation reuses unchanged source and exact exported weights from
the previously audited own B5/ConvNeXt release. Their source provenance, original
training configurations, split manifests and EMA tensor hashes are included.

The detector is an own official-competition-data fine-tune of the generic
Ultralytics YOLO26s model. Ultralytics code/model distribution is subject to its
AGPL-3.0 license or a separately obtained commercial license. No license grant
beyond the upstream terms is asserted here. The exact final S20 EMA inference archive is included, copied unchanged from the audited parent v2 package.

PyTorch, torchvision, timm, segmentation_models_pytorch, OpenCV, NumPy, SciPy,
scikit-learn, joblib and pycocotools remain under their respective upstream
licenses. Exact installed dependency versions are recorded in requirements.txt.
This package contains no competition image pixels or annotation data. The user
must obtain the official competition inputs through their own authorized access.
